Why the WooCommerce Shopify Connector keeps both storefronts in step

Two Way Product SyncImport simple and variable Shopify products with images, variants, prices, and stock, and export WooCommerce products back to Shopify individually, in bulk, or automatically on publish.Real Time Webhook SyncShopify webhooks report order and product changes the moment they happen, and you choose the sync direction plus exactly which product fields each direction is allowed to update.Multiple Shopify StoresConnect as many Shopify stores as your business runs, each with its own sync direction, category mapping, pricing rules, and settings, all managed from one WooCommerce screen.Order Import and ExportBring Shopify orders into WooCommerce with the order status you choose, and push WooCommerce orders to Shopify with one click or automatically when a customer checks out.Fulfillment Tracking UpdatesWhen an order is fulfilled on Shopify, the tracking company, number, and link are written onto the matching WooCommerce order, so your customer sees delivery details without being asked.Store Data ImportMove Shopify customers, coupons, pages, blog posts, tax rates, and shipping zones into WooCommerce, so both storefronts match without anyone retyping content by hand.Category Collection MappingMap WooCommerce product categories to Shopify collections once, then every future import and export files products into the right place on both stores automatically.Flexible Price RulesRaise or lower prices by a fixed amount or a percentage for products inside a chosen price range, applied per connected store during import or export.Sync Performance DashboardTrack products and orders synced, success rates, and daily trends, with an error table that pairs every failure code with a suggested fix, plus CSV export and a daily digest email.WooCommerce Shopify Connector ComparisonSee how this connector compares with S2W, W2S, LitExtension, and Syncio across two way sync, multi store support, real time webhooks, order fulfillment tracking, and licensing model.
