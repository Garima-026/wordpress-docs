## Notes on these videos

Several videos cover **paid add-ons** rather than the core plugin — Split Cart, Quote System, Group Buy, Per Product Shipping, Price Comparison, Buyer Seller Chat, and Web Push Notifications are each sold separately on the [Webkul store](https://store.webkul.com/). The core marketplace features are documented in full under [Documentation](./documentation/).

Videos were recorded against earlier plugin releases, so a screen may not match your version exactly. Where a video and a written guide disagree, follow the [written documentation](./documentation/) — it tracks the current release. For anything still unclear, [raise a ticket](https://webkul.uvdesk.com/).
