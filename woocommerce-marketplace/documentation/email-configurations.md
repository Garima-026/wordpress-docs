# Email Configurations

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/manage-email-listings.webp"><img src="./assets/manage-email-listings.webp" alt="Email Template Settings" /></a>

The WooCommerce Marketplace plugin allows the admin to configure and manage **email templates** used for marketplace-related notifications.

---

## Configuration Path

- Navigate to:
  **WooCommerce > Settings > Emails**

---

## Email Templates Management

- Admin can **customize email templates** for marketplace-related events.
- A hyperlink **“Click here to preview your email template”** is available to preview the email layout.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/webkul-woocommerce-multi-vendor-marketplace-email-template-configuration.webp"><img src="./assets/webkul-woocommerce-multi-vendor-marketplace-email-template-configuration.webp" alt="Email Template Configuration" /></a>

---

## Email Template Preview

- Clicking the preview link displays the **email layout and design**.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/webkul-woocommerce-multi-vendor-marketplace-email-template-preview.webp"><img src="./assets/webkul-woocommerce-multi-vendor-marketplace-email-template-preview.webp" alt="Email Template Preview" /></a>

---

## Available Marketplace Email Templates

The admin can manage multiple marketplace email templates, such as:

- Ask to Admin
- Product Publish
- Vendor Approval
- Vendor Registration
- Other marketplace notification emails

These templates are listed under:

- **WooCommerce > Settings > Emails**

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/webkul-woocommerce-multi-vendor-marketplace-email-listings.webp"><img src="./assets/webkul-woocommerce-multi-vendor-marketplace-email-listings.webp" alt="Email Template Listings" /></a>

---

## Configure Individual Email Template

- Click the **Manage** button next to the required email template.
- The admin can configure:
  - Enable / Disable email
  - Email subject
  - Email heading
  - Email content and additional settings

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/manage-email-listings.webp"><img src="./assets/manage-email-listings.webp" alt="Email Template Settings" /></a>

---

This feature gives the admin **full control over marketplace email communication** with vendors and customers.
