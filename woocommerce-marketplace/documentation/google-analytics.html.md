# Get Google Analytics Measurement ID

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/google-analytics-get-started.webp"><img src="./assets/google-analytics-get-started.webp" alt="Google Analytics Get Started" /></a>

This ID helps you track marketplace activity such as visitor traffic, product views, and user interactions, giving you better insights into how your marketplace is performing.
To get the **Google Analytics Measurement ID**, follow these steps:

---

## Steps to Get Google Analytics Measurement ID

1. **Sign in / Get Started**
   - Go to Google Analytics.
   - Click **Sign in to Analytics** or **Get started today**.

2. **Start Measuring**
   - Click **Start measuring**.
   - Enter account details and click **Next**.
   <a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/google-analytics-start-measuring.webp"><img src="./assets/google-analytics-start-measuring.webp" alt="Google Analytics Start Measuring" /></a>

3. **Create a Property**
   - Enter **Property name**, **Reporting time zone**, and **Currency**.
   - Click **Next**.
   <a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/google-analytics-create-property.webp"><img src="./assets/google-analytics-create-property.webp" alt="Google Analytics Create Property" /></a>

4. **Describe Your Business**
   - Select the option that best describes your business.
   - Click **Next**.
   <a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/google-analytics-business-description.webp"><img src="./assets/google-analytics-business-description.webp" alt="Google Analytics Business Description" /></a>

5. **Select Business Objectives**
   - Choose relevant business objectives.
   - Click **Next**.
   <a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/google-analytics-business-objectives.webp"><img src="./assets/google-analytics-business-objectives.webp" alt="Google Analytics Business Objectives" /></a>

6. **Accept Terms & Conditions**
   - Read and accept the **Terms & Conditions**.
   <a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/google-analytics-terms.webp"><img src="./assets/google-analytics-terms.webp" alt="Google Analytics Terms" /></a>

7. **Choose Platform**
   - Select **Web** as the platform.
   <a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/google-analytics-platform-selection.webp"><img src="./assets/google-analytics-platform-selection.webp" alt="Google Analytics Platform Selection" /></a>

8. **Set Up Data Stream**
   - Set up the web stream.
   - Click **Create & Continue**.
   - Click **Next** after creation.
   <a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/google-analytics-data-stream.webp"><img src="./assets/google-analytics-data-stream.webp" alt="Google Analytics Data Stream" /></a>

9. **Copy Measurement ID**
   - Copy the **Measurement ID** shown on screen.
   - Paste it into the WooCommerce Marketplace module configuration.
   <a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/google-analytics-measurement-id.webp"><img src="./assets/google-analytics-measurement-id.webp" alt="Google Analytics Measurement ID" /></a>

**Note:**
You can add the Google Analytics Measurement ID at a later stage as well, after the marketplace setup and testing are completed. This step is optional during the initial configuration.
