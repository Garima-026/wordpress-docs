# Admin News – Vendor

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/news.webp"><img src="./assets/news.webp" alt="Admin News – Vendor" /></a>

The **Admin News** section allows vendors to stay updated with important announcements shared by the marketplace admin.

---

## View Admin News

- Vendors can view all the news sent by the admin from their vendor dashboard.
- Only news assigned or made visible to vendors will appear, and vendors can click on any news item to read the full details.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/admin-news.webp"><img src="./assets/admin-news.webp" alt="Admin News – Vendor" /></a>

---

## View News Details

- By tapping on any news item, the vendor can view the complete description and details of that news.
- This helps vendors stay informed about marketplace updates, policies, or announcements.

---

## Summary

- Vendors can access admin-published news from their dashboard.
- Each news item opens with full details on selection.
- Ensures clear communication between admin and vendors.
