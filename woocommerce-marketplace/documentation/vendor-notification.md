# Vendor Notification

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/notifications.webp"><img src="./assets/notifications.webp" alt="Vendor Notifications" /></a>


The **Vendor Notification** section keeps vendors informed about important activities and updates in the marketplace.

---

## Vendor Notifications Overview

- Vendors receive notifications for various marketplace actions such as:
  - New orders placed by customers.
  - Changes in order status (processing, completed, refunded, etc.).
  - Product approval or rejection by the admin.
  - Other important marketplace-related updates.

---

## Notification Display

- All notifications are displayed in the **Notification area** of the vendor dashboard.
- Vendors can quickly review updates without navigating to different sections.

**Reference Media:**

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/vendor-notification.webp"><img src="./assets/vendor-notification.webp" alt="Vendor Notifications" /></a>

---

## Benefits

- Keeps vendors informed in real time.
- Helps vendors take quick action on orders and product updates.
- Improves communication between admin and vendors.
