# Vendor Staff

The **Vendor Staff** feature allows vendors to add sub-users (store managers) who can log in and manage the store on their behalf. Vendors can grant per-task permissions for each section (view, add, edit, delete, and more) based on the capabilities allowed by the admin.

---

## Managing Vendor Staff

Vendors can view a list of all their staff members from the Vendor Dashboard.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/vendor-staff-list.png"><img src="./assets/vendor-staff-list.png" alt="Vendor Staff List" /></a>

### Adding a New Staff Member

To add a new staff member, click on the **Add Staff** button. Fill in the required details such as Name, Email, and Password.

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/add-vendor-staff.png"><img src="./assets/add-vendor-staff.png" alt="Add Vendor Staff" /></a>

### Editing Staff Settings and Permissions

Vendors can edit existing staff settings and define precise permissions for what the staff member is allowed to do. 

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/edit-vedor-staff-setting.png"><img src="./assets/edit-vedor-staff-setting.png" alt="Edit Vendor Staff Setting" /></a>

The permissions can be granularly controlled per section (e.g., Products, Orders, Profile) and per action (e.g., view, add, edit, delete).

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/allowed-staff-to-do.png"><img src="./assets/allowed-staff-to-do.png" alt="Allowed Staff Permissions" /></a>

<a class="doc-image-link" href="./assets/allowed-staff-to-do-2.png"><img src="./assets/allowed-staff-to-do-2.png" alt="Allowed Staff Permissions Continued" /></a>

---

This allows vendors to easily scale their operations by delegating tasks to their team members while maintaining strict control over their store's security and data.
