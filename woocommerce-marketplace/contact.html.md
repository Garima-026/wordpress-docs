# Contact

The WooCommerce Multi-Vendor Marketplace plugin is published and supported by **Webkul Software**.

## Support

Support for this plugin is handled through Webkul's ticket system. Licensed customers get support included with their purchase.

- **Support portal:** [webkul.uvdesk.com](https://webkul.uvdesk.com/) — raise a ticket for installation problems, configuration questions, or bug reports
- **Email:** [support@webkul.com](mailto:support@webkul.com)
- **Store contact form:** [store.webkul.com/contacts](https://store.webkul.com/contacts/)

When reporting a problem, include your WordPress and WooCommerce versions, the plugin version, and the exact steps that reproduce it. The [Installation guide](./documentation/installation.html) lists the minimum supported versions.

## Sales and licensing

- **Product page:** [WooCommerce Multi-Vendor Marketplace](https://store.webkul.com/woocommerce-multivendor-marketplace.html) — pricing, license terms, and purchase
- **Live demo:** [wpdemo.webkul.com/woocommerce-marketplace](https://wpdemo.webkul.com/woocommerce-marketplace/) — try the marketplace before buying
- **Add-ons:** payment gateways, shipping methods, tax integrations, mobile apps, and point of sale extensions are listed on the [Webkul store](https://store.webkul.com/)
