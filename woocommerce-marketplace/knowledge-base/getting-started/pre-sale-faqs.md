# Pre-sale FAQs

Short answers to the questions people ask before buying.

## What does the plugin do?

It turns a standard WooCommerce store into a **multi-vendor marketplace**: vendors register, open shop pages, list and manage products, handle their own orders, and request payouts, while you control approval, commissions, and settings. More in [About](https://wpdoc.webkul.com/woocommerce-marketplace/about.html) and [Features](https://wpdoc.webkul.com/woocommerce-marketplace/documentation/features.html).

## What kind of marketplace can I build?

Any multi-seller marketplace modeled on the big platforms. You supply the vendors, catalog, and branding. Common builds include:

- **Amazon**-style general marketplace (many vendors, one catalog)
- **eBay**-style listings marketplace
- **Etsy**-style handmade / creator marketplace
- **Souq / Noon**-style regional marketplace
- **Flipkart**-style multi-category marketplace

The plugin provides the vendor onboarding, shop pages, commissions, and payouts; the look and niche are yours.

## Is there a live demo?

Yes. Try the [live marketplace demo](https://wpdemo.webkul.com/woocommerce-marketplace/) before buying.

## Can I extend it with add-ons?

Yes. Payment gateways, shipping, tax, mobile apps, POS, split cart, quote system and more are sold separately on the [Webkul store](https://store.webkul.com/).

## Is support included?

Yes. Support comes with your purchase through [uvDesk](https://webkul.uvdesk.com/). Please read [Before you open a ticket](../billing-support/before-you-open-a-ticket.html) first.
