Bundle Products for WooCommerce

Sell curated product kits from one product page — fixed or dynamic pricing, customer-selectable optional items, automatic bundle discounts and per-order purchase limits, with every bundle's contents carried through cart, checkout, orders and emails.

Why Bundle Products is the fastest way to sell kits

One bundle product page sells a whole basket — you decide what's inside, what's optional and what it costs; the plugin keeps the math and the stock rules honest.

2 modes
fixed or dynamic pricing
Charge one flat price for the kit, or let the total follow exactly what the customer selects.
Pick & mix
optional bundle items
The first N items are mandatory; customers tick checkboxes to add the rest to their kit.
Live
bundle total & savings
"Bundle Total" and "You Save" update instantly as items are ticked and quantity changes.
% or $
bundle-level discounts
A percentage or fixed-amount discount on the final bundle price, shown struck-through in the catalog.
Max N
per-order purchase limit
Cap how many bundles one order may contain — enforced on the product page, cart and checkout blocks.
Variations
bundleable too
The item search covers simple products and individual variations — bundle "Hoodie, Black, L" exactly.
1 line
from cart to email
Each bundle stays one order line with its contents listed underneath — in cart, checkout, orders and emails.
HPOS
+ block cart & checkout
Declares High-Performance Order Storage compatibility and enforces its rules in the Store API.
0 settings
pages to configure
Everything lives on the product itself — pick the Bundle Product type and one extra tab appears.

What's New in v1.0.0

  • New Bundle Product type in the WooCommerce product editor with a dedicated Bundle Items tab
  • Fixed and dynamic pricing modes with optional percentage or fixed-amount bundle discounts
  • Compulsory Items Count — make the first items mandatory and the rest customer-selectable
  • Max Purchase Limit per order, enforced in the classic cart, mini-cart and block checkout
  • Storefront bundle box with live "Bundle Total" and "You Save" recalculation
  • Bundle contents carried into cart, checkout, orders and order emails
  • Read-only and admin REST API endpoints under product-bundle/v1
