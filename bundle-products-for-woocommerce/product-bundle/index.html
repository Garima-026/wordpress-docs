Bundle Products for WooCommerce

Bundle Products for WooCommerce adds a dedicated Bundle Product type to your store. A bundle is one product page that sells a curated kit of existing products — a washing machine with an optional phone, laptop and TV, a starter pack with mandatory tools and add-on accessories, or any combination you can assemble from your catalog.

You stay in full control of the commercial logic:

  • What's inside — any published simple products, and even specific variations, each with its own quantity.
  • What's mandatory — the first items of the list are always included; the rest become optional checkboxes for the customer.
  • What it costs — one fixed price for the whole kit, or a dynamic total that follows the customer's selection, with an optional bundle discount on top.
  • How many per order — an optional per-order purchase limit, enforced everywhere from the product page to the checkout block.

The big picture

  1. Install the plugin on a store where WooCommerce is active — no settings pages, no setup wizard.
  2. Create a product and switch its type to Bundle Product: a new Bundle Items tab appears.
  3. Add bundle items in order — mandatory items first — and choose a pricing mode, discount and limits.
  4. Publish. The storefront shows the "This Kit/Bundle Includes" box with live totals; customers configure their kit and add it to the cart as one line item.
  5. Fulfil orders as usual — every order line lists the bundled products underneath, in the admin, on the customer's order pages and inside order emails.
