Import product descriptions, images, and technical specifications straight from the Icecat catalog into your WooCommerce store — one product at a time or thousands via CSV.

Why IceCat Connector?

Writing product content by hand is slow, and copying it from manufacturer sites is slower. The WooCommerce IceCat Connector plugs your store into Icecat, the world's largest open product catalog, so a single ID, GTIN, or brand + product code pulls in the full title, descriptions, image gallery, and complete technical specification sheet — ready to sell.

Guided Setup Wizard

Go from a fresh install to a verified Icecat connection in four guided steps — welcome, account, import preferences, and a configuration summary.

Verified Icecat Account

Connect a free Open Icecat username — or add your Full Icecat App Key — and the plugin verifies it against the live Icecat API before any import runs.

Search & Import Products

Find any catalog product by Icecat ID, GTIN, or Product Code & Brand, review the fetched data, and create it as a WooCommerce product in one click.

Learn more

Bulk CSV Import

Upload a CSV of Icecat IDs, GTINs, or product codes and let the connector import everything in the background, with a live progress bar and a failed-items report.

Learn more

Background Image & Attribute Sync

Import galleries and specification attributes in the background so product creation stays fast — or switch to inline import when you want everything at once.

Learn more

Category Mapping

Assign imported products to their original Icecat category automatically, or pick your own WooCommerce categories per import — your store, your taxonomy.

Learn more

One-Click Refresh

Re-pull the latest catalog data for a single product from its edit page, for a selection via bulk update, or for the whole store with Refresh All Products.

Learn more

Manage Imported Products

A dedicated list of every Icecat import — with search, bulk publish, trash, delete, and update actions, plus a direct link back to each product's Icecat page.

Enrich Existing Products

How it works

Install the pluginUpload and activate the connector on your WooCommerce store — the Setup Wizard opens automatically after activation.Connect your Icecat accountEnter your free Open Icecat username (plus an App Key for Full Icecat) and the wizard verifies it against the live catalog.Import & sellSearch by ID, GTIN, or brand & code — or upload a CSV — and products arrive complete with images, descriptions, and spec sheets.
