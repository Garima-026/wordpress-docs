Manage Products

Webkul WC Addons → IceCat Connector → Products → All Products lists every product imported from Icecat, newest first.

What the List Shows

ColumnContent
Product IdThe WooCommerce product ID, with row actions: Edit (opens the WooCommerce product editor), Delete (with confirmation), and Icecat (opens the product's page on icecat.biz).
Product ImageThe featured image (appears once background image import finishes).
Product NameThe imported title.
Icecat id / GTIN / Brand|CodeThe catalog identifiers the product was imported with.
Import DateWhen the product was created from Icecat.
StatusThe WooCommerce status — Published, Pending, Draft, or Trash — as a colored pill.

Use Screen Options (top-right) to change how many rows show per page (default 10), and the Search IceCat ID box to find a product by its catalog ID.

Background image notice

While image jobs are still queued you'll see "Product images are importing in the background. N images left." above the list. It clears automatically when the queue finishes — no action needed.

Bulk Actions

Tick one or more products, choose an action from Bulk actions, and click Apply:

ActionEffect
DeletePermanently deletes the WooCommerce product and its downloaded Icecat images, and removes it from this list. Row-level Delete asks for confirmation first — "This action cannot be undone."
UpdateRe-fetches each selected product from Icecat (in its own import language) and updates its content immediately. Success and failure notices list the affected product IDs.
TrashMoves the WooCommerce product to the trash (recoverable from Products → All Products in WooCommerce).
Product PublishPublishes the selected products and makes them visible in the catalog — e.g. "Product ID:- 546 published successfully!"

Selecting no rows shows "Please select at least one item to perform this action on."

Refresh All Products

The Refresh All Products button (top-right) re-imports the latest Icecat data for every product in the list:

  1. Click the button; a dialog confirms — "WooCommerce products will start updating from Icecat with the latest product details in the background."
  2. Click Refresh Products. The page reloads and a progress card appears.

The card shows "X of Y products refreshed (Z%)" and updates itself every few seconds; when everything is done, a toast confirms "All products have been refreshed successfully." The refresh runs in the background — you can leave the page and come back.
