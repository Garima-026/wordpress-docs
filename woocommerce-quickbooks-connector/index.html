Connect your WooCommerce store with Intuit QuickBooks Online and keep customers, products, orders, payments, refunds, and taxes in sync — automatically.

Get Started →Buy Now

Why QuickBooks Connector?

Bookkeeping by hand is where store hours go to die. The WooCommerce QuickBooks Connector links your store directly to Intuit QuickBooks Online, so every customer, product, order, payment, and refund can flow into your accounting company without copy-paste — in real time or on your schedule.

Guided Setup Wizard

Go from a fresh install to a connected QuickBooks company in four guided steps — environment, credentials, authorization, and a live connection test.

Learn more

Secure OAuth 2.0 Connection

Authorize with Intuit in one click. The Company ID, access token, and refresh token are fetched automatically and can be re-authorized any time.

Learn more

Product Export & Import

Export products — variations included — to QuickBooks, or stage QuickBooks items in an Import List and create them in WooCommerce with duplicate-SKU protection.

Learn more

Customer Sync

Import QuickBooks customers into WooCommerce or export store customers to your books, with a searchable per-customer mapping dropdown.

Learn more

Order Export & Tracking

Export orders manually or automatically by status as a Sales Receipt, Invoice, Estimate, or Credit Memo — and track sync state from every order list.

Learn more

Payments & Transaction Fees

Record payments per gateway when orders are received or completed, sync gateway fees with the right VAT, and schedule weekly batch bank deposits.

Learn more

Refund Receipts & Voids

A WooCommerce refund can create a matching refund receipt in QuickBooks automatically, and cancelled orders can void their QuickBooks transaction.

Learn more

Tax Class Mapping

Map every WooCommerce tax class to the matching QuickBooks tax rate — with a default tax code fallback — so exported totals always reconcile.

Learn more

Account & Default Mapping

Choose the QuickBooks asset, income, expense, and deposit accounts, the default tax code, import batch size, and product preferences.

Learn more

How it works

Install the pluginUpload and activate the connector on your WooCommerce store — it takes a minute from the Plugins screen.Connect with the wizardPick Sandbox or Production, paste your Intuit app keys, and authorize with one click — the wizard tests the connection for you.Configure & syncMap your QuickBooks accounts and taxes, switch on Auto Sync, and let customers, products, orders, and payments flow.
