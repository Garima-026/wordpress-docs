Auto Sync Settings

Auto Sync turns the connector from a manual tool into a background service: products, orders, payments, refund receipts, and images synchronize as your store operates.

Open Webkul WC Addons → WC Quick Book Connector → Configuration → Auto Sync.

Product Export Sync (WooCommerce to QuickBooks)

SettingDescription
WooCommerce to QuickBook Real Time SyncEnable to export a product to QuickBooks automatically whenever it is published or updated
Select Categories for Auto Sync of ProductsLimit real-time export to chosen WooCommerce categories — pick All or any set of parent/child categories

A product outside the selected categories is skipped (the skip is noted in the sync log). Variable products export each variation.

Product Import Sync (QuickBooks to WooCommerce)

SettingDescription
QuickBook To WooCommerce Real Time SyncEnable to create/update WooCommerce products when items change in QuickBooks (delivered via QuickBooks webhooks)
Select Categories for Auto Sync of ProductsLimit real-time import to chosen QuickBooks categories

Imported products receive the default status chosen in General Settings.

Order & Payment Sync

SettingOptionsDescription
Auto Export OrdersDisable, All, Pending, Processing, On hold, CompletedExports an order the moment it reaches the selected status (with All, every status change syncs). Works together with the transaction type set in Export Order Settings
Payments to QuickbooksDisable / EnableMaster switch for payment records; fine-tune per gateway in Payment Methods → Advanced Configuration
Auto Generate Refund ReceiptDisable / EnableCreates a QuickBooks refund receipt whenever you refund a WooCommerce order

How auto export fires

Order export triggers both on the customer's order-received page and on any admin status change — whichever brings the order to your selected status first.

Image Sync

SettingOptions
Add/Update Image on QuickBooks/WooCommerceDisabled, WooCommerce to QuickBooks, QuickBooks to WooCommerce

Product images follow the chosen direction whenever a product is added or updated.

Click Save Auto Sync Settings to apply.

Behind the Scenes

  • QuickBooks change notifications (items, invoices, bills, payments) arrive by webhook and are processed asynchronously so they never slow your site down. For this, register your store's webhook endpoint in your Intuit app — see Set up the webhook.
  • Background schedules re-check recently updated QuickBooks items every few minutes and work through the pending update queue.
  • Every sync event is written to WooCommerce → Status → Logs (look for the WooCommerce QuickBook log sources) — the first place to check if something didn't sync.
