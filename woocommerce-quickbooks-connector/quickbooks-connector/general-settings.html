General Settings

General Settings tells QuickBooks where your commerce data belongs — which accounts receive product income and expenses, which tax code applies by default, and how imports behave. Complete this page right after connecting.

Open Webkul WC Addons → WC Quick Book Connector → Configuration → General Settings.

QuickBooks Account Mapping

Each dropdown lists the matching accounts from your connected QuickBooks company:

SettingDescription
Asset AccountQuickBooks asset account used for inventory products
Default Expense AccountExpense account used when syncing products
Default Income AccountIncome account recorded for product sales
Default Deposit to AccountAccount that receives deposits by default

Map accounts before syncing products or orders

Product and order exports reference these accounts. If the dropdowns are empty, check that the connection is active — the lists load live from QuickBooks.

Tax & Import Batch Settings

SettingDescriptionAllowed values
Default Tax CodeQuickBooks tax code applied when no specific tax mapping matchesYour QuickBooks tax codes
Batch Size For ImportHow many records each import batch processes3 – 30

A smaller batch size is gentler on slow servers; a larger one finishes big imports faster.

Product Preferences

SettingDescriptionOptions
Default QuickBooks Import Product StatusWordPress status given to products created from QuickBooksDraft, Pending, Published
Export Products on QuickBooks With Price ZeroWhether zero-price products are exportedNo / Yes

Review imports as drafts

Importing as Draft lets you review titles, prices, and images before products go live on your storefront.

Click Save Account to apply your changes.
