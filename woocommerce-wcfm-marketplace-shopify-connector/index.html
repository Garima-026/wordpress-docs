Why the WCFM Shopify Connector is the complete vendor sync toolkit

Give every vendor their own Shopify connection inside the WCFM dashboard, and keep products, orders, stock and prices aligned in both directions.

Real Time Sync
Shopify webhooks keep price, quantity and SKU current in both directions, and carry fulfilment tracking back to the shopper without anyone pressing a button.
Guided Setup Wizard
A four step wizard takes each vendor from store name to a verified, authorised Shopify connection, so no developer is needed to get a store online.
Multiple Shopify Stores
Every vendor can connect more than one Shopify account using OAuth or a legacy custom app, then switch between them from any tab with independent settings.
Import Products
Pull Shopify products into WooCommerce with variants, images, prices and stock, staged in two steps so nothing is created until the vendor has seen what was fetched.
Export Products
Push a vendor's own WooCommerce products out to the Shopify catalogue, in bulk or one row at a time, with export status tracked on every line.
Import and Export Orders
Bring Shopify orders in as WooCommerce orders with the status you choose, and send marketplace orders out to Shopify by hand or the moment they are placed.
Collection to Category Mapping
Fetch the Shopify collections, pair each one with a WooCommerce category, and decide whether imports cover the whole catalogue or only the collections you mapped.
Price Rules
Adjust prices automatically inside a price band on import or export, by a percentage or a fixed amount, without ever touching the source catalogue.
Administrator Oversight
See every vendor connection on one screen, check whether real time sync is actually working, and disconnect an account cleanly when a vendor leaves.
WCFM Shopify Connector Comparison
See how the connector compares with W2S Sync, the CedCommerce Shopify Connector and Syncio on marketplace support, vendor self service, two way sync, price rules, documentation and licensing before you choose.

What's New in v3.0.0

Added

  • New guided Setup Wizard for connecting a Shopify account, covering store information, API credentials and one-click connect
  • Redesigned interface for Account List, Add and Manage Account, Map Categories, Price Rule and Settings, and the vendor product and order tables
  • Product and order import, export and image sync are now processed through Action Scheduler
  • JavaScript strings are now translatable
  • Confirmation popups before deleting records

Updated

  • Table views and pagination for the product, order and category listings
  • Compatible with WooCommerce 11.0
  • Compatible with WordPress 7.1
  • Security hardening and coding standards updates

Fixed

  • Real-time product sync (price, stock and status) and image sync issues
  • Product import and export, and order import and export, edge cases with missing or malformed Shopify data
  • Stuck background processing
