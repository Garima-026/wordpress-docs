Let every seller in your WooCommerce marketplace run their Shopify store and their marketplace shop as one catalogue — products, collections, orders, stock and prices moving in both directions, from a single screen inside My Account.

Why Marketplace Shopify Connector?

A seller who already trades on Shopify and joins your marketplace normally has to maintain two catalogues by hand — enter every product twice, keep the prices matched, and copy each order across so stock stays honest. The WooCommerce Marketplace Shopify Connector removes that duplication. The seller connects their store once, decides which way each kind of data should flow, and the connector keeps both sides current.

Guided Setup Wizard

Go from a fresh install to a verified Shopify connection in four guided steps — welcome, store details, credentials, and a confirmation summary.

Multiple Shopify Accounts

A seller can connect more than one Shopify store, switch between them from the header, and give each its own settings, mappings and price rules.

Import Products

Pull simple and variable products with images, price, SKU, stock, weight and every option, then create them in WooCommerce with the status you choose.

Export Products

Push the seller’s own products into the Shopify catalogue — all at once, a selection, or one row at a time — and update them without creating duplicates.

Learn more

Import & Export Orders

Bring Shopify orders into WooCommerce with the status you pick, and create marketplace orders on Shopify by hand or automatically at checkout.

Learn more

Category Mapping

Fetch the Shopify collections, pair each one with a WooCommerce category, and choose whether imports cover the whole store or only the mapped collections.

Learn more

Price Rules

Add or subtract a fixed amount or a percentage inside a price band, in whichever direction you sync, without touching the source catalogue.

Learn more

Live Webhook Sync

Product updates, deletions and order fulfilments arrive as signed Shopify webhooks and are applied to WooCommerce by a background queue.

How it works

Create the Shopify appCreate an app in the Shopify Developer Dashboard for the Client ID and Secret, or use an existing legacy custom app’s access token.Connect the storeThe seller runs the four-step wizard from My Account. It verifies the credentials against Shopify and registers the webhooks before it finishes.Map, import, exportPair collections with categories, pull the Shopify catalogue in, push marketplace products out, and let orders flow both ways.

What's New in v3.0.0

  • Guided setup wizard — A four-step wizard connects a Shopify store from My Account — it takes the credentials, verifies them against Shopify and registers the sync webhooks before it finishes. Setup Wizard →
  • Export All Products and Export All Orders — Send an entire catalogue or order book to Shopify in one action, alongside the existing per-row Export to Shopify button and the bulk-action dropdown. Exporting Products →
  • Lighter product updates — Images are re-downloaded only when they actually change, and a failed download no longer leaves a product without pictures. An update rewrites just the variations that changed rather than every one. Importing Products →
  • Counts of what is still waiting — The Import Product tab shows how many staged Shopify products are still to be created and how many images the background queue has left, so you can tell at a glance whether an import has finished. Importing Products →
  • Webhooks removed with the account — Deleting a Shopify account now also deletes that account’s webhook subscriptions from Shopify, so a disconnected store stops sending updates instead of leaving orphaned webhooks behind. Webhooks & Auto-Sync →
