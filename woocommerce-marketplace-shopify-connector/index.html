Why Webkul Marketplace Shopify Connector is the complete two way sync toolkit

Close the gap between a seller's own Shopify store and your WooCommerce marketplace with one connector that moves data in both directions.

Real Time Two Way Sync
Product updates, deletions and order fulfilments arrive as signed Shopify webhooks and are applied to WooCommerce by a background queue that recovers from a stall.
Guided Setup Wizard
Go from a fresh install to a verified Shopify connection in four guided steps — welcome, store details, credentials, and a confirmation summary.
Multiple Shopify Accounts
A seller can connect more than one Shopify store, switch between them from the header, and give each its own settings, mappings and price rules.
Import Products
Pull simple and variable products with images, price, SKU, stock, weight and every option, then create them in WooCommerce with the status you choose.
Export Products
Push the seller's own products into the Shopify catalogue — all at once, a selection, or one row at a time — and update them without creating duplicates.
Import and Export Orders
Bring Shopify orders into WooCommerce with the status you pick, and create marketplace orders on Shopify by hand or automatically at checkout.
Collection to Category Mapping
Fetch the Shopify collections, pair each one with a WooCommerce category, and choose whether imports cover the whole store or only the mapped collections.
Price Rules
Add or subtract a fixed amount or a percentage inside a price band, in whichever direction you sync, without ever touching the source catalogue.
Field by Field Reference
Exactly which product and order fields travel in each direction, how product type and stock are decided, and which fields an update is allowed to write.
WooCommerce Marketplace Shopify Connector Comparison
See how the connector compares with CedCommerce Shopify Dokan Integration, Smackcoders W2S Sync and Syncio on marketplace support, seller self-service, two way sync, price rules, documentation and licensing before you choose.

What's New in v3.0.0

  • Guided Setup Wizard — a four step wizard connects a Shopify store from My Account, verifies the credentials against Shopify and registers the sync webhooks before it finishes
  • Export All Products and Export All Orders — send an entire catalogue or order book to Shopify in one action, alongside the per row export button and the bulk action dropdown
  • Lighter Product Updates — images are re-downloaded only when they actually change, and an update rewrites just the variations that changed rather than every one
  • Pending Counts on Import — the Import Product tab shows how many staged products are still to be created and how many images the background queue has left
  • Webhook Cleanup on Deletion — deleting a Shopify account now also deletes that account's webhook subscriptions from Shopify instead of leaving orphaned webhooks behind
