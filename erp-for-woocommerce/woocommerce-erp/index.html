ERP for WooCommerce

ERP for WooCommerce adds a complete warehouse and operations back office to your WooCommerce store. Your team signs in at yourstore.com/erp and manages everything operational in one place — warehouses, bin-level inventory, order fulfilment, supplier purchasing and stock transfers — while WooCommerce keeps doing what it does best: selling.

Who is this documentation for?

You are…Start here
A store owner setting the plugin upInstallation, then Roles & Access Control
An administrator configuring warehousesWarehouse Management and the Layout Builder
A warehouse manager running daily operationsERP Dashboard and Order Fulfilment
Purchasing staffRFQ Management and Purchase Orders

The big picture

  1. Set up — install the plugin, grant your team access, create warehouses and build their bin layout.
  2. Stock up — assign your WooCommerce product stock into bins (or receive it through purchase orders).
  3. Sell & fulfil — paid orders automatically enter the fulfilment workflow: validate → reserve → pick → pack → ship.
  4. Replenish — send RFQs to suppliers, convert approved quotes to purchase orders, and receive goods into bins.
  5. Rebalance — move stock between warehouses with internal transfers.

Continue with the Overview to understand the key concepts, or jump straight to Installation.
