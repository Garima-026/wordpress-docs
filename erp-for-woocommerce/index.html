Run warehouses, bin-level inventory, purchasing and guided order fulfilment for your WooCommerce store — from one back office at yourstore.com/erp.

Why ERP for WooCommerce is the control room of your store

WooCommerce tells you how many units you own. The ERP tells you where they sit, who reserved them, and what happens to them next.

Bin-level
stock accuracy
Every unit tracked to the exact zone, aisle, rack, shelf and bin it physically sits in.
Guided
order fulfilment
Order Received → Validated → Picking → Packing → Shipping — one step at a time, no stages skipped.
1,920
locations in one run
The Layout Builder creates an entire warehouse structure from a five-step wizard.
PDF
quotations & receipts
Email suppliers a branded RFQ, convert approved quotes to purchase orders in one click.
QR
coded bins
Print a QR label for every bin — receiving and transfers accept scanned codes.
Live
role-aware dashboard
Stock health, at-risk products, the fulfilment pipeline and purchasing — at a glance.
3 roles
+ per-module access matrix
Managers, warehouse staff and suppliers each see exactly what you allow — nothing more.
8-stage
internal transfers
Move stock between warehouses with approval, picking, dispatch, receiving and damage accounting.
28
email notifications
Customers and staff stay informed automatically at every workflow step.

What's New in v1.0.0

  • ERP back office at /erp with a live, role-aware Dashboard
  • Warehouses with a five-level location hierarchy, capacities, bin QR codes and a visual warehouse graph
  • Layout Builder — bulk-create an entire warehouse layout in five steps
  • Stock assignment to bins with automatic capacity checks and a movement Activity Log
  • Five new order statuses with item-level validate / reserve / pick / pack / ship actions
  • RFQ → Purchase Order purchasing flow with PDF documents and goods receipts
  • Internal transfers between warehouses with dispatch and receiving sessions
  • Access Control matrix and three new user roles
  • 28 WooCommerce email notifications
