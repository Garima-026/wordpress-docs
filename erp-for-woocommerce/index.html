Why ERP for WooCommerce is the control room of your store

WooCommerce tells you how many units you own. The ERP tells you where they sit, who reserved them, and what happens to them next.

Bin Level Stock Accuracy
Track every unit to the exact zone, aisle, rack, shelf and bin where it physically sits, so counts stay reliable and picking never starts with a search.
Guided Order Fulfilment
Paid orders move through validation, picking, packing and shipping one step at a time, so no stage is skipped and every order leaves the warehouse checked.
Warehouse Layout Builder
Create an entire warehouse structure in one run with a five step wizard, generating up to 1,920 locations instead of adding each one by hand.
RFQ and Purchase Orders
Email suppliers a branded request for quotation as a PDF, convert approved quotes into purchase orders in one click and receive goods straight into bins.
QR Coded Bin Labels
Print a QR label for every bin so receiving and transfers accept scanned codes, cutting typing errors during putaway and stock movement.
Role Aware Dashboard
See stock health, products at risk, the fulfilment pipeline and purchasing activity at a glance, with each WooCommerce ERP role viewing only what applies to them.
Roles and Access Control
Managers, warehouse staff and suppliers each get their own role, and an access matrix controls exactly which modules every role can read or change.
Internal Stock Transfers
Move stock between warehouses through approval, picking, dispatch, receiving and damage accounting, keeping both sides of the transfer accurate at all times.
Automatic Email Notifications
28 email notifications keep customers, staff and suppliers informed automatically at every step of fulfilment, purchasing and stock transfers.

What's New in v1.0.0

  • ERP back office at /erp with a live, role aware Dashboard
  • Warehouses with a five level location hierarchy, capacities, bin QR codes and a visual warehouse graph
  • Layout Builder to create an entire warehouse layout in five steps
  • Stock assignment to bins with automatic capacity checks and a movement Activity Log
  • Five new order statuses with item level validate, reserve, pick, pack and ship actions
  • RFQ to Purchase Order purchasing flow with PDF documents and goods receipts
  • Internal transfers between warehouses with dispatch and receiving sessions
  • Access Control matrix and three new user roles
  • 28 WooCommerce email notifications
