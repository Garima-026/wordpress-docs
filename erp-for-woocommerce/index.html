ERP for WooCommerce

Run warehouses, inventory, purchasing and order fulfilment for your WooCommerce store from one back office.

Get Started →Features

🏭 Multi-Warehouse Management

Create warehouses with a full zone → aisle → rack → shelf → bin hierarchy, capacities and QR-coded bins.

📦 Bin-Level Inventory

Place WooCommerce stock into exact bins, track every movement in a complete audit ledger.

🛒 Guided Order Fulfilment

Orders flow through Order Received → Stock Validated → Picking → Packing → Shipping → Completed.

🔒 Stock Reservation

Validate and reserve stock per order line so two orders can never claim the same units.

📄 RFQs & Purchase Orders

Email suppliers a PDF quotation request, approve their offer and convert it into a purchase order.

🚚 Internal Transfers

Move stock between warehouses with approval, picking, packing, dispatch and receiving steps.

👥 Roles & Access Control

Warehouse Managers, Warehouse Staff and Suppliers, each limited by a per-module permission matrix.

✉️ 28 Email Notifications

Customers and staff are kept informed at every workflow step through WooCommerce emails.

Why ERP for WooCommerce?

WooCommerce tells you how many units you own. ERP for WooCommerce tells you where they are, who reserved them, and what happens to them next. The plugin adds a dedicated ERP back office at yourstore.com/erp where your team manages warehouses, assigns stock to bins, fulfils orders step by step, buys from suppliers and moves goods between locations.

Warehouse Management

Model each physical facility as zones, aisles, racks, shelves and bins — one by one, or thousands at a time with the Layout Builder wizard. Every bin gets a unique code and a printable QR code.

Read the Warehouse Guide →

Order Fulfilment

Every paid order enters a guarded workflow. Staff validate stock, reserve exact bins, pick, pack and ship — and the plugin blocks anyone from skipping a stage by accident.

Read the Fulfilment Guide →

Purchasing

Out of stock? Create a Request for Quotation from the order itself, email the supplier a branded PDF, and convert the approved quote into a purchase order you receive straight into bins.

Read the RFQ Guide →

Internal Transfers

Rebalance stock between warehouses with a complete request → approve → pick → pack → dispatch → receive lifecycle, including partial-quantity negotiation and damage reporting.

Read the Transfers Guide →
