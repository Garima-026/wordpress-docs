Services, Extras and Fees

Two separate tabs, for two different jobs:

TabUse it forTypical example
ServicesThings the customer chooses to addAirport pickup, breakfast, an extra hour
Extras / FeesThings you charge, whether they choose them or notA city tourism levy, a cleaning fee, a late-checkout option

Both appear in the booking panel on the product page and both feed straight into the live price.

Services

The tab opens with the service settings panel; the list of services is beneath it.

Click Add service and fill:

FieldNotes
Service nameWhat the customer sees — Airport pickup
PriceThe charge
How customer chooses itOptional checkbox, Customer chooses quantity, or Based on booking duration
Vendor ID (optional)For marketplace setups. Leave 0 otherwise
RequiredRequired services are always added
Hidden from customerHide it from the form but keep it in the system
Min quantity / Max quantityOnly shown for the quantity type
DescriptionOptional explanation
Sort orderLower numbers appear first

The three ways a service can be chosen

TypeWhat the customer seesGood for
Optional checkboxA tick box with the price beside itAirport pickup, a welcome pack
Customer chooses quantityA number box between your minimum and maximumBreakfasts per guest, extra towels
Based on booking durationPriced from the length of the bookingAn add-on that scales with nights or hours

On the storefront they appear grouped under your Frontend Services Label (default Services):

Extras and fees

The tab opens with the extras settings panel; the list of extras and fees is beneath it.

Click Add extra/fee and fill:

FieldNotes
Extra / fee nameCity tourism fee, Late checkout
TypeFee or Extra — a label for your own organisation
AmountThe figure
Amount typeFixed, Percent of base, Per person, Per day, Per night
RequiredRequired charges are pre-ticked and cannot be removed
Hidden from customerKeep it out of the form
ConditionWhen it applies — see below
Condition JSONThe condition's value
DescriptionOptional explanation
Sort orderLower numbers appear first

Amount types

Amount typeCalculated as
FixedA flat figure
Percent of baseA percentage of the base booking amount
Per personMultiplied by the guest count
Per dayMultiplied by the number of days
Per nightMultiplied by the number of nights

Conditions

A condition decides whether the extra applies at all. Each takes a small JSON value — copy the placeholder in the form and change the numbers.

ConditionCondition JSONMeaning
Always{}Applies to every booking
Minimum amount{"threshold":200}Only when the booking is worth at least 200
Minimum persons{"min":2}Only from two guests up
Minimum nights{"min":2}Only from two nights up
Specific dates{"dates":["2026-06-01"]}Only on those dates
Specific months{"months":[6,7]}Only in those months
Specific weekdays{"days":[0,6]}Only on those weekdays (0 Sunday … 6 Saturday)

Important

The JSON must be valid or the record is refused with "Please fill required fields." Keep the braces, quotes and commas exactly as shown.

On the storefront extras appear under your Frontend Extras Label (default Fees and extras). Required ones are pre-ticked and marked with an asterisk:

Fees and extras
  ☐ Late checkout          +₹500.00
  ☑ City tourism fee *     +₹150.00

Services or extras — which should I use?

QuestionAnswer
Can the customer decline it?Yes → Service. No → Extra/Fee marked Required
Does it only apply in certain circumstances?Extra/Fee with a condition
Does the customer choose how many?Service with Customer chooses quantity
Is it a percentage of the booking?Extra/Fee with Percent of base
Is it charged per night or per person?Extra/Fee with Per night or Per person

Worked examples

Breakfast at ₹350 per guest, optional Service · price 350 · Customer chooses quantity · min 1 · max 6.

A ₹150-per-night tourism levy nobody can opt out of Extra/Fee · type Fee · amount 150 · amount type Per night · Required · condition Always.

A 10 % cleaning surcharge, only on bookings of three nights or more Extra/Fee · amount 10 · amount type Percent of base · condition Minimum nights · condition JSON {"min":3}.

A weekend supplement Extra/Fee · amount type Fixed · condition Specific weekdays · condition JSON {"days":[0,6]}.

Checking your work

  1. Open the product on your storefront.
  2. Confirm the two groups appear with the labels you chose.
  3. Tick a service and watch the Estimated price rise by the right amount.
  4. Confirm required fees are already ticked and cannot be cleared.
  5. Book a date where a conditional extra should not apply and confirm it is absent.

Everything the customer selected is carried through to the cart, the checkout and the order line item — see The Customer Booking Experience.
