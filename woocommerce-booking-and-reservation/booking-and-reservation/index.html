WooCommerce Booking and Reservation

WooCommerce Booking and Reservation turns products in your existing catalogue into things people can reserve: a room for three nights, a 30-minute appointment, a van for the weekend, a seat at Saturday's tour, or a four-hour block on a piece of equipment. The customer picks a date on a calendar, chooses guests, rooms and add-ons, sees the price update as they go, and checks out through your normal WooCommerce cart.

Everything you configure and everything you run day to day lives on a single admin screen — Webkul WC Addons → Booking Management System — split into four tabs: Booking Dashboard, Bookings, Product Settings and Global Settings.

This documentation is written for store owners and the people who run the bookings desk. It follows the way the extension is actually used, not the way it is built.
