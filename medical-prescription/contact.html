Contact

The Medical Prescription Attachment for WooCommerce plugin is published and supported by Webkul Software.

Support

Support for this plugin is handled through Webkul's ticket system. Licensed customers get support included with their purchase.

  • Support portal: webkul.uvdesk.com — raise a ticket for installation problems, configuration questions, or bug reports
  • Email: support@webkul.com
  • Store contact form: store.webkul.com/contacts

When reporting a problem, include your WordPress and WooCommerce versions, the plugin version, your active theme, whether you are using the classic cart/checkout or the Cart/Checkout Blocks, and the exact steps that reproduce it. The Installation guide lists the minimum supported versions, and FAQ & Troubleshooting covers the problems reported most often — a missing prescription label, an empty Allowed Categories list, and a 404 on the My Account tabs.

If the problem involves an uploaded prescription, do not attach a real customer's prescription to a support ticket. Reproduce it with a dummy file instead; a real one carries patient health data.
