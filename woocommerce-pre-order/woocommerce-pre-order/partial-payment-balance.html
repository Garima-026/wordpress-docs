Partial Payments & Balance Collection

WooCommerce Pre-Order supports both full payments upfront and flexible partial payments (deposit model). This guide explains how deposits are calculated, how outstanding balances are tracked, and how payment gateways collect the final balance automatically or manually.

1. Deposit Models

When configuring a pre-order product, you can enable Partial Payment. The customer pays an initial booking fee (deposit) at checkout to reserve the product, while the remaining balance is deferred until the product becomes available.

Deposit Calculation Types

  • Fixed Deposit: The customer pays a set, static price upfront (e.g., $10) to reserve the item.
  • Percentage Deposit: The customer pays a percentage of the regular product price upfront (e.g., 20%). The plugin calculates this dynamically.
  • Remaining Balance: The outstanding balance is calculated automatically as: $$\text{Remaining Balance} = \text{Regular Price} - \text{Deposit Paid}$$ This outstanding balance is recorded in the customer's pre-order history.

2. Automated Stripe Balance Charging

For stores using the official WooCommerce Stripe Gateway, the WooCommerce Pre-Order plugin includes an automated, hands-off balance collection system. This system bills the customer's card automatically when the product is restocked, removing the need for customers to return to the site and complete checkout.

How the Automated Process Works:

  1. Secure Card Saving: During pre-order checkout, the system securely saves the customer's card details in your Stripe account. The customer is notified that their card will be charged when the item launches.
  2. Product Release: When stock arrives and you update the product status to In Stock, the system triggers the automatic charging process.
  3. Smooth Batch Processing: To prevent your server from overloading or timing out when processing thousands of orders, the plugin bills credit cards in batches of 20 orders at a time behind the scenes.
  4. Automatic Invoices & Receipts:
    • If the charge succeeds, a new balance order is automatically created, marked as paid, and a receipt email is sent to the customer.
    • If the charge fails (e.g., card expired, insufficient funds), the system automatically flags the order and emails the customer an invoice with a manual checkout link.

3. Manual Balance Payment Flow

For payment methods that do not support automated card saving (such as PayPal, bank transfers, or check payments), or if an automated Stripe charge fails, the customer can complete their payment manually:

  1. Restock Email Notification: The system automatically sends a "Back-in-Stock" notification email to the buyer once the product is restocked.

  2. Direct Payment Link: This email contains a checkout link that takes the buyer directly to the payment page.

  3. Customer Dashboard: Customers can also log into their account, go to My Account > Pre-Orders (or use the OTP guest page), locate the pending pre-order, and click Pay Balance.

  4. Standard Checkout: The customer is redirected to the checkout page containing only the balance due, where they can choose any active store payment method to complete their order.
