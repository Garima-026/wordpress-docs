Pre-Order Campaigns

Pre-order campaigns allow merchants to run coordinated pre-sales for groups of products. You can schedule campaigns with start/end dates, set custom discount rules, and display the products on any storefront page using built-in shortcodes.

To manage campaigns, navigate to WooCommerce > Pre-Order List and select the Pre-Order Campaigns tab.

1. Creating a Campaign

Click Add New Campaign to open the Campaign Editor.

The editor is split into three configuration tabs:

Tab A: General Information

  • Campaign Name: The internal name for the campaign (e.g., “Fall Tech Launch 2026”).
  • Banner Text (Description): Promotional text displayed to customers. You can use dynamic merge tags in this block:
    • {name} — The Campaign Name.
    • {discount} — The formatted discount amount (e.g. 15% off).
    • {start_date} — Formatted start date/time.
    • {end_date} — Formatted end date/time.
  • Start & End Dates: Enter local times for the campaign launch and closure. The plugin converts these inputs to UTC for precise scheduling.
  • Shortcode: Once a campaign is saved, the editor displays its unique shortcode:
    [wk_campaign_products campaign_id="YOUR_CAMPAIGN_ID"]
    Paste this shortcode on any WordPress page, Gutenberg block, or Elementor container to render the campaign product grid. The grid only displays while the campaign is actively running.

Tab B: Discount & Pricing

Configure how the campaign's promotional pricing interacts with individual product catalog prices:

  • Discount Type: Choose Percentage or Fixed Amount.
  • Discount Value: Enter the numerical value (e.g., 15 for a 15% markdown).
  • Discount Precedence: If a product in the campaign already has a custom pre-order discount defined at the individual product level, determine which discount takes priority:
    • Higher Wins: The plugin compares the campaign discount and the per-product discount, applying whichever saves the customer more.
    • Campaign Always Wins: The campaign discount overrides all product-level values.

Tab C: Products

Attach or detach products to the campaign.

  • Add Products: Opens a search box powered by Select2. Search your WooCommerce catalog, select one or multiple products, and click Attach to Campaign.
  • Remove Selected: Check the boxes next to assigned products and click to detach them.

2. Campaign States & Lifecycle

A campaign shifts automatically through states based on its scheduled dates and active toggle status:

  • Draft: The campaign has been saved but is not active. Products do not show campaign pricing.
  • Scheduled: Saved and activated, but the start date is in the future. Shop loop badges show "PRE-ORDERS START SOON!" to build anticipation.
  • Active: The current date falls within the campaign schedule window. Campaign discounts are applied, and countdown timers are visible on product pages.
  • Expired: The end date has passed. Pre-ordering automatically closes, returning items to their standard shop status.
  • Disabled: Manually paused or turned off by the admin, pausing all campaign rules regardless of schedule.

3. Sidebar Quick Metrics

The right-hand sidebar of the Campaign Editor provides quick administrative oversight:

  • Campaign Status Pill: Color-coded indicator displaying the active status.
  • Duration: The scheduled length of the campaign.
  • Days Remaining: A real-time countdown of remaining days until expiration.
  • Quick Stats: Total number of products currently assigned and the active discount rate.
