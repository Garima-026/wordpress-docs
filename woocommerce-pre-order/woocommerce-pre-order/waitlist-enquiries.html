Waitlists & Enquiry Threads

The plugin provides two distinct customer communication tools to capture shopper interest and resolve customer questions directly on your pre-order product pages:

  1. Back-in-Stock Waitlist ("Notify Me"): Shoppers can subscribe to automated email alerts that notify them when an out-of-stock product returns to stock.
  2. Pre-Order Enquiry System ("Ask Query"): Shoppers submit detailed questions, initiating threaded support chats directly with store administrators.

1. Back-in-Stock "Notify Me" Waitlist

When a pre-order product sells out, or when a standard catalog item runs out of stock, the plugin displays a Notify Me button next to the standard purchase layout.

The Subscription Flow

  • For Logged-In Customers: The user subscribes with a single click. The plugin retrieves their account name and email automatically.
  • For Guest Shoppers: Clicking the button opens a modal popup where guests enter their Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
  • Preventing Duplicates: If a customer submits their email multiple times for the same product, the system updates their subscription date rather than creating duplicate records, ensuring they only receive a single email notification when the item returns.

How Notifications are Sent

When you restock the product (updating its status to In Stock in WooCommerce):

  1. The plugin searches for all waitlisted subscribers for that product.
  2. The system automatically sends a personalized email alert to all subscribers using the Pre-Order Back in Stock mail template.
  3. Subscribers are marked as notified so they are not emailed multiple times.

2. Pre-Order Enquiry System

The Pre-Order Enquiry System allows shoppers to submit questions regarding release dates, pricing, or product specifications before committing to a purchase.

Submitting a Query

If the Enquiry Form is enabled in your configuration:

  • An Ask Query button appears on the pre-order product page.
  • Clicking the button opens a popup form asking for:
    • Name (Pre-filled for logged-in users)
    • Email (Pre-filled for logged-in users)
    • Phone Number (Optional)
    • Subject / Question (Textarea)
  • Spam Prevention: Submissions have built-in security and rate limits (lockouts after rapid submissions) to block automated spam bots from flooding your inbox.

Threaded Conversations

Administrators view and manage inquiries under WooCommerce > Pre-Order List > Enquiries tab.

  • Chat Interface: Clicking an inquiry opens the conversation panel.

    Administrators can:

    • Review past messages chronologically in a chat bubble format.
    • Change the thread status (e.g. from Pending to Closed).
    • Write a reply inside the editor and click Send Reply.

  • Customer Notification: Sending a reply automatically emails the customer with the admin's response, keeping them updated without requiring them to check the site.
