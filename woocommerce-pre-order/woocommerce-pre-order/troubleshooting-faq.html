Troubleshooting & FAQ

This section provides solutions to common issues encountered during configuration, integration, or store operations, along with answers to frequently asked questions.

1. Common Issues & Resolutions

A. Pre-Order Button Not Appearing on Product Page

Symptoms: The product displays a standard "Add to Cart" or "Out of Stock" button instead of "Pre-Order Now."

  • Check Start Date: If you configured a Start Date, the pre-order buttons will only display after that timestamp. If the start date is in the future, the standard out-of-stock or add-to-cart layout is preserved.
  • Check Status Toggle: Verify that the product-level Pre-Order Status toggle is set to Enabled.
  • Stock Conflicts: If you checked Automatic Pre-Order (Auto-convert when out of stock), the product must be set to Out of Stock in WooCommerce. If the product has physical stock (stock quantity > 0), it will display standard purchase buttons.
  • Theme Template Overrides: Custom product page templates may omit standard WooCommerce hooks (woocommerce_before_add_to_cart_button, woocommerce_single_product_summary). Revert to standard Storefront or Twenty Twenty-Four themes to isolate template conflicts.

B. Stripe Automated Balance Charges Not Executing

Symptoms: Pre-order products are restocked, but customer cards are not automatically billed for the remaining balance.

  • Stripe Webhook Connection: Automated payments require Stripe to communicate with your website. Check your Stripe Dashboard settings to verify that webhooks are active and successfully delivering events to your store.
  • WordPress Task Backlog: The plugin queues automatic payments using standard background tasks. If payments are delayed, verify that your server's background task runner (cron) is working normally.
  • Saved Payment Method: Auto-charging only functions if the customer's payment details were securely saved at the initial checkout. For guests or users who opted out of saving their card, they must pay the balance manually.

C. Guest Users Cannot Access their Pre-Orders

Symptoms: Guests see an error message or do not receive their email verification code.

  • Verify "My Pre-Orders" Page: Make sure the My Pre-Orders page exists in your WordPress admin panel under Pages > All Pages. If it is missing, go to WooCommerce > Pre-Order List > Configuration > Guest Settings and save the configuration to recreate it.
  • Check Order Association: For security, the system only sends verification codes to emails that have active pre-orders. Double-check that a pre-order was placed with the exact email address the visitor is typing in.
  • Host or Cookie Blocks: Some strict caching plugins or firewalls blocks custom cookie sessions. Verify that guest session tracking is excluded from your server's cache or optimization plugins.

2. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I set different pre-order rules for individual variations?

Yes. Under the Product Data box of a variable product, you can configure pre-orders on the parent level (which applies to all variations) or expand individual variations under the Variations tab to set separate availability dates, deposit structures, and prices per variation.

How are taxes and shipping calculated on partial payments?

  • Taxes: Taxes are calculated during both stages of the transaction. The initial checkout calculates taxes on the partial deposit amount. The second checkout (balance payment order) taxes the remaining balance.
  • Shipping: Shipping fees are charged during the initial pre-order checkout. The balance payment order does not charge shipping again, avoiding double charges.

What happens if a customer misses the completion window?

If Days to Complete is configured (e.g., 7 days) and a customer fails to pay their remaining balance before the deadline:

  • The daily Action Scheduler sweep runs.
  • If Auto-Cancel Overdue Pre-Orders is enabled in the configuration, the pre-order status is changed to Cancelled, the inventory is returned to general stock, and a cancellation notice is sent.
  • If auto-cancel is disabled, the customer is marked as Overdue, but the order remains active for manual administrator resolution.

Is the plugin compatible with High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS)?

Yes. The plugin is fully compatible with WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS), ensuring maximum order search speeds and stable database operations for modern storefronts.
