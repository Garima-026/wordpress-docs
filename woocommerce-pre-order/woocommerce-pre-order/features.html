Key Features & Specifications

Explore the complete feature set of WooCommerce Pre-Order (v3.2.0). Use this list to check if your store's marketing, checkout, and operations match the plugin's capabilities.

1. Pre-Order Configuration Options

  • Global Pre-Order Enablement: Enable pre-ordering globally across the entire catalog or restrict it to specific categories.
  • Per-Product Controls: Override settings at the individual product level.
  • Variable Product Support: Configure separate pre-order statuses, dates, and prices for individual product variations.
  • Pre-Order Status Indicators: Custom labels displayed on the shop page, product page, cart, and checkout indicating the item's pre-order status.

2. Flexible Pricing & Deposits

  • Full Payment upfront: Collect 100% of the product cost at the initial checkout.
  • Fixed Deposit: Require customers to pay a fixed deposit (e.g., $10) to reserve the product.
  • Percentage Deposit: Set a deposit rate (e.g., 20%) calculated dynamically from the item price.
  • Pay on Arrival: Allow customers to complete bookings with zero upfront payment, paying the full amount only when the product becomes available.
  • Balance Automatic Charging: Charge the customer's Stripe card automatically once the item goes in-stock.
  • Manual Balance Invoicing: Send automatic emails with direct payment links for customers to pay the balance via the standard WooCommerce checkout.

3. Dynamic Pre-Order Campaigns

  • Schedule Launch Dates: Set start and end dates/times for pre-orders. When the end date is reached, the product automatically transitions to a standard catalog product.
  • Stock Limits per Campaign: Cap pre-order sales. Once reached, pre-ordering closes.
  • Page Customization: Show countdown timers to build urgency.
  • Timer Styles: Select from Flip, Digital, or Simple countdown formats.

4. Waitlists & Notifications ("Notify Me")

  • Auto-Waitlist for Out of Stock: Display a "Notify Me" button on regular out-of-stock product pages.
  • Email Subscription Log: Customers enter their email address to subscribe to back-in-stock alerts.
  • Automatic Back-In-Stock Alerts: Once stock quantity is updated in admin, the system automatically emails waitlisted subscribers.
  • Waitlist Management Panel: View, filter, and delete waitlist subscribers in the admin panel.

5. Guest Pre-Orders & Tracking

  • Guest Checkout Enablement: Allow non-registered guest users to purchase pre-order items.
  • One-Time Password (OTP) Verification: Protect guest order access. To track orders or pay a balance, a guest enters their email and receives a secure 6-digit OTP code to access their temporary "My Pre-Orders" dashboard.
  • Secure Access Keys: Built-in validation limits request counts to prevent spam and protect shopper privacy.

6. Admin Control & Analytics

  • Orders List: Filter pre-order transactions by status (Paid, Pending Balance, Completed).
  • Enquiry Management System: View and reply directly to customer pre-order queries from the admin dashboard.
  • Sales Analytics: Built-in dashboard showing pre-order conversion rates, revenue collected, deposits pending, and campaign metrics.
  • Bulk Actions: Change pre-order status, release dates, or notify subscribers in bulk.

7. Pre-Order Customer Inquiries

  • On-Page Inquiry Form: Displays an "Inquiry" tab or button on pre-order product pages, allowing prospective buyers to submit questions.
  • Admin Discussion Threads: Allows administrators to reply to customer questions from a central panel, maintaining a clear messaging history.
  • Email Notifications: Automatically notifies customers via email when an administrator answers their question.
