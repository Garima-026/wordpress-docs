Introduction

Welcome to the WooCommerce Pre-Order Plugin documentation. This guide details how to implement, configure, and maintain the WooCommerce Pre-Order plugin to run automated pre-sales and back-in-stock notifications in your WooCommerce store.

[!NOTE] This documentation is written for WooCommerce Pre-Order Version 3.2.0 and is fully compatible with WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS) and the modern Cart & Checkout block architectures.

What is WooCommerce Pre-Order?

In a standard e-commerce store, when a product runs out of stock or is not yet released, the store owner loses potential revenue, and customer engagement drops. WooCommerce Pre-Order transforms out-of-stock states into valuable sales opportunities.

This plugin allows merchants to:

  1. Sell Unreleased Products: Build buzz and collect capital prior to launch by scheduling pre-order campaigns.
  2. Collect Partial Deposits: Increase checkout conversions by letting customers pay a small booking fee upfront, with the remaining balance collected upon product release.
  3. Automate Balance Collection: Integrate with payment gateways like Stripe to automatically charge saved cards when products go in-stock.
  4. Offer Guest Waitlists: Enable non-registered users to secure pre-orders or subscribe to notifications using secure verification OTP codes.
  5. Manage Stock Waitlists ("Notify Me"): Gather waitlists for standard out-of-stock items, sending automated back-in-stock alerts to subscribers.
