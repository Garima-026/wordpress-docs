Guest Pre-Order Access (OTP Portal)

The plugin provides a secure, passwordless Guest Pre-Order Dashboard that allows guest customers (non-registered users) to track their pre-orders, check outstanding balance payments, and pay their remaining balance when products are released.

1. Setting Up Guest Access

For the guest dashboard to function on your store, two conditions must be met:

  1. Enable WooCommerce Guest Checkout: Standard WooCommerce guest checkout must be active. Go to WooCommerce > Settings > Accounts & Privacy and check “Allow customers to place orders without an account”.
  2. Enable Guest Pre-ordering: Go to WooCommerce > Pre-Order List > Configuration > Guest Settings and enable “Guest Pre-ordering”.

The "My Pre-Orders" Page

Upon activation, the plugin automatically creates a page in your WordPress site titled My Pre-Orders (located at the web link /my-pre-order-list). This page displays a secure verification form. If you accidentally delete this page, simply save the settings under the guest configuration tab again to automatically recreate it.

2. How Guest Verification Works

Because guests do not have password-protected accounts, they log in using a secure Email One-Time Password (OTP). The login flow is fast and easy for customers:

Security & Privacy Rules

To protect customer privacy and prevent spam, the portal operates under the following safeguards:

  • Rate Limits: Customers can request up to 5 login codes within 15 minutes per email. Requests beyond this limit are temporarily paused to block automated spam.
  • Expiration Window: Each verification code is valid for exactly 10 minutes before it expires.
  • Privacy Protection: If a visitor enters an email address that has never placed a pre-order, the system will not send an email but will display the standard confirmation screen. This prevents random visitors from discovering which emails have placed orders on your store.
  • One-Time Use: A code is immediately invalidated once it is successfully used to log in.

3. The Guest Dashboard View

Once verified, the guest customer is logged into their session (which defaults to 30 minutes before requiring a new code) and sees a clean list of their pre-orders:

ColumnDescription
OrderThe parent WooCommerce order number.
ProductThe name of the pre-ordered item.
QuantityThe quantity purchased.
Remaining AmountThe outstanding balance due.
StatusShows Pending, Completed, Cancelled, or Ready — balance due.
ActionAction buttons such as Complete Final Order (displays when balance is ready to pay).

4. Completing the Balance Payment as a Guest

When a pre-ordered product is restocked by the admin, guest buyers can pay their remaining balance:

  1. The customer logs into the My Pre-Orders page using an email code.
  2. A Complete Final Order action link appears next to the ready item.
  3. Clicking the link adds the product to the shopping cart and automatically redirects the customer to the checkout page.
  4. The checkout page displays the remaining balance instead of the full price. The customer fills in their details and uses any available payment gateway to pay the balance and complete their purchase.
