Seamlessly bridge the gap between your physical inventory and WooCommerce digital backend.

Visual
drag & drop label creator
Visually layout product names, prices, SKUs, and custom barcode properties in real time.
Auto-Print
fulfillment automation
Hook into woocommerce_thankyou to trigger hands-free bag/box label prints right when orders complete.
ZPL
raw vector stream parsing
Convert layout properties into raw ZPL command streams for crystal-clear thermal printing.
